Plan a usability test for:

{subject}

Participants: {users}

Use usability-testing and interaction-design.

Produce:
- What you are trying to learn, specifically.
- Realistic tasks, phrased as goals rather than instructions.
- What you will observe: where they hesitate, backtrack, or misread.
- What you will not say, to avoid leading them.
- Success criteria per task, defined in advance.
- How many participants and why that number.
- How findings will be recorded and prioritised.

Rules: give goals, not steps, or you test instruction-following. Do not
ask whether they like it; observe whether they succeed. Note where you
would be tempted to intervene and stay silent. Small samples find most
usability problems but do not measure preference.
