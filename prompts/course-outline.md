Outline a course on:

{subject}

Format: {format}

Use curriculum-sequencing and learning-plan.

Produce:
- The capability learners have at the end, stated concretely.
- Prerequisites, honestly.
- Modules in dependency order, each with its learning outcome.
- Where practice sits relative to instruction.
- A working end-to-end example early, before depth.
- Assessment: how a learner knows they have it.
- What is deliberately excluded.

Rules: sequence by what is learnable, not by topic taxonomy. Get to
something working early rather than covering foundations exhaustively.
Every module needs practice and a way to self-check. State what the
course does not cover.
