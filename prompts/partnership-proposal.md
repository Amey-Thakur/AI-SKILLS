---
name: partnership-proposal
description: Propose a partnership stating what each side gains, with a small first step rather than a broad commitment.
variables:
  - "{partner}: who they are, what they do, and why they would care"
  - "{opportunity}: what you want to do together and what you bring"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Draft a partnership proposal for:

{partner}

Opportunity: {opportunity}

Use agent-partnerships-desk.

Structure:
- Why them, specifically, referencing something real about their business.
- What they gain, stated as concretely as what you gain.
- The smallest viable first step, not a broad partnership.
- What each side contributes and by when.
- How success would be measured after the first step.
- What happens if it does not work.

Rules: their benefit must be as specific as yours or the proposal reads
as a request. Propose one small step rather than an open-ended
relationship. Do not imply commitments you cannot make. Any resulting
agreement needs legal review, particularly exclusivity or revenue terms.
