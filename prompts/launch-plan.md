name: launch-plan
description: Plan a launch with readiness checks, sequenced channels, and a defined follow-through after the spike.
variables:
  - "{launch}: what is launching, to whom, and when"
  - "{readiness}: current state of product, docs, support, and infrastructure"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."
---

Plan the launch of:

{launch}

Readiness: {readiness}

Use launch-marketing and product-launch, plus capacity-planning for the
traffic.

Produce:
- The single goal of the launch.
- Readiness checklist with owners: product, docs, pricing, support,
  infrastructure.
- Channel sequence and timing, warm audience first.
- Briefing for support and sales, including likely objections.
- What happens to people who arrive and do not convert.
- Rollback or pause criteria if something breaks.
- What is measured, and when the review happens.

Rules: do not launch to traffic the system cannot serve. Every asset must
exist before announcement. State the single goal rather than several. Plan
the week after, since attention decays fast.
