Write positioning for:

{product}

Alternatives: {alternatives}

Use positioning-and-messaging and competitive-strategy.

Produce:
- The primary audience, specifically rather than broadly.
- The alternative you are replacing.
- The outcome the buyer gets, not the mechanism.
- One differentiator you can defend and evidence for it.
- A one-sentence statement to use everywhere.
- Three supporting proof points.

Rules: pick one audience, since positioning for everyone reaches nobody.
The differentiator must be one a competitor could not equally claim. Use
words buyers would use rather than internal vocabulary. State what you
are choosing not to be.
