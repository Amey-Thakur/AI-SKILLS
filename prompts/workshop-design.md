---
name: workshop-design
description: Design a workshop where participants do the work rather than watch a presentation.
variables:
  - "{outcome}: what participants will be able to do afterwards"
  - "{group}: how many, their starting level, and how long you have"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Design a workshop for:

{outcome}

Group: {group}

Use curriculum-sequencing, teaching-technical-concepts, and
deliberate-practice.

Produce:
- The capability participants leave with, stated observably.
- A timed agenda, majority spent doing rather than listening.
- Exercises that build on each other, each with a checkable result.
- Materials and setup needed in advance.
- Checkpoints for whether people are keeping up.
- What to cut if you run short.
- Follow-up so it does not evaporate.

Rules: participants must be doing within the first fifteen minutes. Every
exercise needs a visible success condition. Plan what to cut, since
workshops always overrun. Prerequisites must be tested before the day.
