name: design-slide-deck
description: Turn an argument into a deck with one idea per slide and headlines that carry the point.
variables:
  - "{argument}: what you are arguing and the evidence for it"
  - "{context}: audience, time available, and the decision wanted"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Build a deck for:

{argument}

Context: {context}

Use slide-deck-creation and storytelling-with-data.

Produce:
- The narrative in sentences before any slides.
- Slide by slide: headline stating the point, and what is on it.
- Which slides carry a chart and what it shows.
- Speaker notes with what is said rather than read.
- The appendix: detail held back for questions.
- Where the ask sits.

Rules: headlines state the point rather than the topic. One idea per
slide. If a slide needs a paragraph, it is two slides or speech. Charts
show the comparison that matters, not every number.
