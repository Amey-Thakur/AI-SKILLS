  - "{role}: the role, the team, and what they will own"
  - "{context}: the systems, the people to meet, and current work in flight"

Build an onboarding plan for:

{role}

Context: {context}

Use agent-people-ops-desk, contributor-onboarding, and knowledge-transfer.

Produce:
- Access and setup needed before day one.
- A first task shippable in week one, chosen to teach the system.
- Weeks one to four, each with an outcome.
- Who they meet and why, in what order.
- What to read, ordered, and what to skip for now.
- Check-in points and what is assessed at each.
- Who is their buddy and what that person does.

Rules: get them to a real contribution in the first week, since that
builds confidence faster than reading. Prerequisites must be tested
before day one. Order reading rather than handing over everything.
Assessment is about support needed, not judgement.
