name: product-strategy
description: Set product direction by choosing who to serve and what not to build, with the bet stated explicitly.
variables:
  - "{context}: the product today, its users, and what is working or not"
  - "{constraints}: team size, runway, competition, and any fixed commitments"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."
---

Set product strategy for:

{context}

Constraints: {constraints}

Use competitive-strategy, product-discovery, and agent-strategy-review.

Produce:
- Who you are serving and who you are explicitly not.
- The problem you are solving better than the alternatives.
- The bet: what you believe that others do not, stated so it can be wrong.
- What you will build, in priority order.
- What you are deliberately not building.
- What would tell you the bet is wrong, and by when.

Rules: strategy requires saying no, so name what is excluded. State the
bet as a falsifiable belief rather than an aspiration. Do not list every
opportunity; choose. Say what evidence would change the strategy.
