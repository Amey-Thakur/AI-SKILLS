variables:
  - "{question}: what you need to learn and the decision it informs"
  - "{participants}: who you will speak to and their relationship to the problem"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."

Write an interview guide for:

{question}

Participants: {participants}

Use customer-interviews and product-discovery.

Produce:
- The learning goal, and what you would do differently based on it.
- Opening questions that establish context without leading.
- Questions about specific past behaviour rather than hypotheticals.
- Probes for the last time they had this problem and what they did.
- Questions that could disconfirm your assumption.
- What not to ask, and why.

Rules: ask about past behaviour, not future intentions, since stated
intent predicts poorly. Never pitch during discovery. Avoid leading
questions and mark any that risk it. Include questions that could prove
you wrong.
