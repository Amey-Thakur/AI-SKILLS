---
name: stakeholder-map
description: Identify who is affected by a decision, what each needs, and how they should be engaged.
variables:
  - "{initiative}: the decision or project and its scope"
  - "{organization}: the teams, roles, and any known tensions"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."
---

Map stakeholders for:

{initiative}

Organisation: {organization}

Use stakeholder-management and cross-team-coordination.

For each stakeholder:
- Their interest: what outcome they are accountable for.
- Their influence: what they can approve, block, or ignore.
- What they need to hear and when.
- Likely objection and the honest answer to it.
- Consulted before the decision, or informed after.

Then give the engagement sequence and who must be spoken to first.

Rules: distinguish the person affected from the person who can block.
Identify likely opposition candidly rather than assuming alignment. Note
where two stakeholders want incompatible things, since that is the real
decision. Consultation that cannot change anything is not consultation.
