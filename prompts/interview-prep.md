Prepare for this interview:

{role}

Background: {background}

Use technical-interviews and system-design-answer.

Produce:
- What the role really needs, read from the description and context.
- Your strongest evidence for each requirement, as specific stories.
- Gaps, and how to address them honestly.
- Questions likely to be asked, and the structure of your answers.
- Questions to ask them, that reveal what you need to know.
- What would make you decline the role.

Rules: prepare evidence rather than claims, with specifics including
what you did versus what the team did. Address gaps honestly rather than
deflecting. Interviews are two-way, so include what you need to learn.
Do not fabricate experience.
