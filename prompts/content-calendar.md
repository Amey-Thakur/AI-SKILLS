name: content-calendar
description: Plan a content calendar from real audience questions, at a cadence that can be sustained.
variables:
  - "{audience}: who reads it and what they are trying to do"
  - "{capacity}: how much can genuinely be produced per period"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Plan a content calendar for:

{audience}

Capacity: {capacity}

Use content-marketing-strategy and agent-content-newsroom.

Produce:
- Themes derived from questions the audience actually asks.
- A schedule that fits the stated capacity, not an aspirational one.
- For each piece: the question it answers, the intent stage, and the
  next step for the reader.
- Which pieces are substantial and which are lighter.
- How each will be distributed, not just published.
- What is measured and when it is reviewed.

Rules: cadence must be sustainable, since consistency matters more than
volume. Every piece answers a question someone has. Include distribution
effort in the capacity, since publishing is not reaching. Do not plan
more than can be produced well.
