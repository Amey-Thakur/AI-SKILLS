variables:
  - "{objections}: the objections heard, or the deal context if they are anticipated"
  - "{product}: what you sell, its real limitations, and its pricing"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."
---

Prepare objection handling for:

{objections}

Product: {product}

Use negotiation-fundamentals and competitive-messaging.

For each objection:
- What is really being asked underneath it.
- The honest answer, including where they are right.
- Evidence that supports the answer.
- When to concede rather than counter.
- What disqualifies the deal rather than being handled.

Rules: never coach around a genuine limitation; state it and say who the
product is not for. Answers must be truthful and verifiable. Price
objections are often value or timing objections, so separate them. If the
honest answer is that they should buy something else, say that.
