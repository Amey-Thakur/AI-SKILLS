---
name: career-plan
description: Build a career plan from a concrete target role, with the gaps named and evidence to be gathered.
variables:
  - "{current}: current role, strengths, and what you have shipped"
  - "{target}: the role or capability you want, and by when"
settings: "Temperature 0.3."
---

Build a career plan for:

Current: {current}

Target: {target}

Use skill-assessment, self-directed-learning, and promotion-packet.

Produce:
- The target described as capabilities, not a title.
- An honest gap assessment against those capabilities.
- What evidence would demonstrate each capability.
- Work to seek out that produces that evidence.
- What to learn, and how you would know you had.
- A realistic timeline with checkpoints.
- What is outside your control.

Rules: base the plan on demonstrable capability rather than time served.
Be honest about gaps, since a plan built on flattery achieves nothing.
Distinguish what you control from what depends on others. Name where
external feedback is needed to calibrate.
