name: seo-content-brief
description: Brief a piece of content that serves the searcher's intent first and search engines second.
variables:
  - "{topic}: the topic and the query the reader would type"
  - "{context}: what your product does and what expertise you can bring"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Write a content brief for:

{topic}

Context: {context}

Use technical-seo and content-marketing-strategy.

Produce:
- The search intent behind the query, and what a satisfied reader gets.
- The angle only you can credibly take.
- An outline with headings mapped to sub-questions.
- What the piece must include to be genuinely useful.
- Internal links that make sense for the reader.
- Title and description written for a human first.
- How success is measured beyond ranking.

Rules: serve intent rather than keyword density. Do not recommend
producing content on a topic where you have no genuine expertise. State
where the piece would compete with existing content you already have.
Thin content ranks briefly and converts nothing.
