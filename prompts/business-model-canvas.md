---
name: business-model-canvas
description: Lay out how a business creates, delivers, and captures value, and check the pieces are consistent with each other.
variables:
  - "{business}: what it does, for whom, and how it charges today or intends to"
settings: "Temperature 0.4."
---

Map the business model for:

{business}

Use the business-model-design skill.

Cover:
- The customer segment, specifically, and the job they are hiring this for.
- The value proposition, stated as the outcome they get.
- Channels: how they find it and how it reaches them.
- Revenue model and why it matches how value accrues.
- Key resources and activities the business must own.
- Cost structure and what drives it.

Then test consistency: does the channel support the price point, does the
cost structure work at the stated price, does the segment have the
willingness to pay assumed.

Rules: name the inconsistencies rather than presenting a tidy canvas.
State which pieces are assumptions rather than known. If the model only
works at a scale the business has not reached, say so.
